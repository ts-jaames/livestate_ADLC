# M6 — Break-from-tradition points aren't legible against the SDLC teams already know

## Risk
Statement: Break-from-tradition points aren't legible against the SDLC teams already know. Under pressure, teams default back to the familiar traditional pattern.
Category: Usability
Gate: Define
Area: Definition (meta)

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that with ten minutes to pick the next delivery move, people who know SDLC will pick the traditional next step — unless the ADLC option is labeled with the SDLC step it replaces and the proof that stands in for that step.
Success criteria: When the ADLC option is labeled that way, at least 4 of 5 pick it and can name the SDLC step it replaces. When it is not labeled, the same people (or a matched 5) pick the traditional step.
Failure criteria: Even with the label, 2 or more of 5 still pick the traditional step. Or they pick the ADLC step but cannot name which traditional step it replaces.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: A short tabletop. Not a real client delivery. One page: "You have 10 minutes to say what the team does next. Leadership wants a start date." Two cards. Traditional: open a delivery phase / start build. ADLC: run one named smallest test against one claim. First pass: cards have no SDLC mapping. Second pass: the ADLC card says which SDLC step it replaces and the one-sentence proof bar. Record the choice and whether they can name the replaced step. Do not turn this into a training program.
Type: Usability
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 5 people. 10 minutes unlabeled, then 10 minutes labeled. Same visit.

## Signal
Success signal: Unlabeled: most pick traditional. Labeled: 4 or more of 5 pick ADLC and correctly name the replaced SDLC step.
Failure signal: With the label, 2 or more of 5 still pick traditional, or pick ADLC without naming the replaced step.
Threshold: If the labeled pass fails, change how we name the break against SDLC. Do not assume ADLC words are enough. If the contrast works, the mapping is what made it usable. Do not promote a full change program off this one test.
