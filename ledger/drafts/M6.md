# M6 — Break-from-tradition points aren't legible against the SDLC teams already know

## Risk
Statement: Break-from-tradition points aren't legible against the SDLC teams already know. Under pressure, teams default back to the familiar traditional pattern.
Category: Usability
Gate: Define
Area: Definition (meta)

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that under a 10-minute time box to pick the next delivery move, people who know SDLC will choose a traditional phase-shaped next step unless the ADLC alternative is labeled with the SDLC step it replaces and the proof bar that substitutes for that step.
Success criteria: In the labeled condition, at least 4 of 5 participants choose the confidence-gated next step and can point to the SDLC step it replaces. In the unlabeled condition, the same people (or a matched 5) choose the traditional step — showing the break is not legible without the mapping.
Failure criteria: Even with the SDLC mapping label, 2 or more of 5 still choose the traditional step, or choose the ADLC step but cannot name which traditional step it replaces.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: One pressured tabletop, not a real client delivery. Scenario card (one page, no new product): "You have 10 minutes to say what the team does next; leadership wants a start date." Two next-step cards: (T) open a delivery phase / start build; (A) run one named slice against one assumption. Run unlabeled first (cards titled only T/A with method text, no SDLC mapping). Then the same five people get card A relabeled as "replaces [named SDLC step] — proof bar is [one sentence from a Define draft]." Record choice and whether they can name the replaced step. Disposable; do not ship a training program from this sitting.
Type: Usability
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: n=5; 10 minutes unlabeled + 10 minutes labeled; same sitting.

## Signal
Success signal: Unlabeled: majority pick T. Labeled: ≥4 of 5 pick A and correctly name the replaced SDLC step.
Failure signal: Labeled condition still has ≥2 of 5 picking T, or picking A without naming the replaced step.
Threshold: Failure on labeled → Iterate how breaks are named against SDLC; do not assume "just use ADLC words." Success on the contrast → mapping is what makes the break usable. Do not Promote a full change-management program from this slice.
