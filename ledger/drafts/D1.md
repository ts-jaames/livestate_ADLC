# D1 — Discovery produces narrative, not testable assumptions

## Risk
Statement: Discovery produces narrative, not testable assumptions. Build begins on unvalidated ground; nothing can be checked.
Category: Usability
Gate: Run
Area: Discovery

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that after a discovery packet, three people who did not write it can each pull at least three claims that are testable (a clear pass and a clear fail) — and that those claims are in the packet, not invented in the meeting.
Success criteria: 3 of 3 readers each list ≥3 claims. Two scorers agree each claim has a visible pass and fail. None of the three lists is only story, journey, or vision language.
Failure criteria: Any reader lists fewer than 3 testable claims. Or 2 or more of the listed items have no visible fail. Or the packet has no claims and readers have to make them up.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: Use one real discovery artifact from this firm (unknown: which engagement — pick one that already exists; do not write a new deck). Three people who did not author it get 20 minutes. They write claims only. Two scorers mark each line testable / story. No coaching on Evidence Work words beyond: "What would we check, and what would count as wrong?"
Type: Usability
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 3 readers, 2 scorers, 20 minutes. One existing packet. Throwaway notes.

## Signal
Success signal: All three readers land ≥3 testable claims that two scorers accept. No one has to invent the claim.
Failure signal: Any reader under 3, or the packet is story-only.
Threshold: Fail → do not treat that discovery as a green light to build. Change the packet until claims are in it. Pass → this sample of discovery can be checked. That is not proof every engagement will do it.
