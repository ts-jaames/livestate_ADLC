# D3 — No confidence gate defined for discovery→build

## Risk
Statement: No confidence gate defined for discovery→build. Work advances because time passed, not because uncertainty dropped.
Category: Operational
Gate: Run
Area: Discovery

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that if people are given a written discovery→build gate (what must be proven, and who records promote / iterate / stop) they will not start build on a date alone — they will point to a named claim that passed.
Success criteria: In a 10-minute tabletop, 4 of 5 refuse to start build when the only reason on the card is a date or "discovery week is over," and they name a missing proof. When the card shows one claim with a pass, 4 of 5 say build may start on that claim only.
Failure criteria: 2 or more of 5 start build from the date card. Or they start build on the pass card for the whole system, not the one claim.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: Two cards, same five people. Card 1: "Discovery ends Friday. Start build Monday." Card 2: "Claim X passed its bar; claims Y and Z have not been tested." They write: do we start build, and on what. No glossary lecture. People who already know SDLC. Not a real client.
Type: Operational
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 5 people. 10 minutes. Throwaway cards.

## Signal
Success signal: ≥4 of 5 block on the date card and limit build to the passed claim on card 2.
Failure signal: ≥2 of 5 start build because time passed, or treat one passed claim as the whole system.
Threshold: Fail → do not call discovery done because the week ended. Write the gate in the open. Pass → this group will wait for proof on paper. That is not proof they will wait under a contract date.
