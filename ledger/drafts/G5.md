# G5 — Stakeholders / clients can't perceive the difference the ADLC makes

## Risk
Statement: Stakeholders / clients can't perceive the difference the ADLC makes. No perceivable difference = no commercial or positioning value. Directly tied to the progress-surface problem below.
Category: Value
Gate: Define
Area: Governance & Commercial

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe someone who did not design this board, given five minutes on it and no lesson, can name at least one difference from a normal status report that is about what we have proven or which gate we are in.
Success criteria: 4 of 5 people name a difference two readers both count as that kind of difference — proven vs promised, footing, or gate. Not a timeline, "on track," or percent done.
Failure criteria: 2 or more of 5 only name calendar or status differences, or cannot name a difference.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: Five people who can stand in for a client. Unknown whether a real client will see this. If not, use people inside the firm who did not write the board, and say so. Each person uses the live board for five minutes, no guide. They write: "What is different from a status report?" Two readers score each answer. If they disagree, a third reader breaks the tie.
Type: Value
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 5 viewers. 2 readers. About 45 minutes. Do not rewrite the board during the test.

## Signal
Success signal: 4 or more of 5 answers count as a real difference, with both first readers (or two of three after a tie) agreeing.
Failure signal: 2 or more of 5 count as ordinary status language, or no difference.
Threshold: If it fails, change the board until the difference is visible. Do not claim commercial or positioning value. If it passes, people can see a difference in this sample. That is not proof they would pay. Do not mix price into this test.
