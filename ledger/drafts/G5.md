# G5 — Stakeholders / clients can't perceive the difference the ADLC makes

## Risk
Statement: Stakeholders / clients can't perceive the difference the ADLC makes. No perceivable difference = no commercial or positioning value. Directly tied to the progress-surface problem below.
Category: Value
Gate: Define
Area: Governance & Commercial

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that a stakeholder who did not design the client board, given 5 minutes on the live surface and no glossary lecture, can state at least one difference from a typical status or %complete report that a second independent scorer agrees is ADLC-specific (footing, gate, what is proven vs promised).
Success criteria: 4 of 5 participants produce at least one difference that both scorers code as ADLC-specific using a pre-agreed codebook (footing / confidence / gate / proven vs empty / slice — not timeline, on track, percent complete, or next milestone).
Failure criteria: 2 or more of 5 produce only calendar/status differences, or cannot name a difference.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: Five people who are "client-like" for this pilot (unknown: whether a real client will see this — use internal people who were not authors if no client is available; mark sample as internal if so). Each uses the live board for 5 minutes, unfacilitated. They write: "What is different from a status report?" Two scorers independently code each answer ADLC-specific / generic-status / none. Disagreements go to a third scorer.
Type: Value
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: n=5 viewers; 2 scorers; ~45 minutes total; no copy rewrite during the sitting.

## Signal
Success signal: ≥4 of 5 answers coded ADLC-specific by both primary scorers (or 2-of-3 after tie-break).
Failure signal: ≥2 of 5 coded generic-status or none.
Threshold: Failure → Iterate the surface until difference is perceivable; do not Promote commercial/positioning claims. Success → difference is perceivable in this sample; commercial value is still a later claim (do not bundle pricing into this slice).
