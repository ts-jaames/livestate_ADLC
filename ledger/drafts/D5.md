# D5 — Client expects 'discovery complete' as the deliverable

## Risk
Statement: Client expects 'discovery complete' as the deliverable. Tension with an evidence-gated exit the ADLC must reconcile.
Category: Value
Gate: Sell
Area: Discovery

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe a buyer or buyer-proxy, shown two exits — "discovery complete" (a deck, system defined) vs "three claims tested, rest still open" — will still pick "discovery complete" as what they would pay for unless the evidence exit also names what they can decide next.
Success criteria: 4 of 5 pick "discovery complete" when the evidence exit has no next decision named. When the evidence exit adds one sentence ("you can choose build / wait / stop on claim X"), 4 of 5 pick that exit or say both are acceptable.
Failure criteria: Even with a named next decision, 2 or more of 5 still require "discovery complete" as the thing they buy. Or they never wanted "discovery complete" even on the first pass (then this risk is not live in this sample).

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: Five people who can stand in for a buyer (unknown: no real client required; say if they are internal). Two one-page offers. No sales script. They pick which they would buy as the discovery deliverable, and write why. Run the version without a next decision first, then the version with one named decision.
Type: Value
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 5 people. 15 minutes. Paper offers only.

## Signal
Success signal: First pass: most want "complete." Second pass: ≥4 of 5 accept the evidence exit once a next decision is named.
Failure signal: Named decision still loses to "complete," or "complete" was never the demand.
Threshold: Fail → selling evidence-gated discovery still fights the buyer's deliverable. Do not assume the method is what they buy. Pass → naming the next decision is what makes the exit sellable in this sample. That is not a signed SOW.
