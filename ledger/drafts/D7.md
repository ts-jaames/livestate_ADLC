# D7 — Lifecycle stops at initial build delivery (no Day-2)

## Risk
Statement: Lifecycle stops at initial build delivery (no Day-2). Ignoring prompt drift, injection/security, and eval harnesses after handoff means the client fails post-offboarding — a firm-reputation collapse, not an engagement one.
Category: Operational
Gate: Support
Area: Discovery

## Assumption(s)
### A1
Statement: We believe that a discovery→build exit with no named Day-2 owner will leave prompt-drift checks unassigned, so no one can say who watches the live agent after handoff.
Success criteria: 4 of 5 readers of that exit cannot name a person or role for "who watches drift after we leave." When a one-line owner is added, 4 of 5 name that owner.
Failure criteria: Even with no owner line, 2 or more of 5 still invent a default owner (then the gap is not visible). Or the owner line is ignored.

### A2
Statement: We believe the same exit, with no injection/security item, will be scored "ready to hand off" by people who would not score it ready if a single abuse case were listed as untested.
Success criteria: Unmarked exit: ≥4 of 5 say ready to hand off. With one untested abuse case listed: ≥4 of 5 say not ready.
Failure criteria: The abuse case does not change the ready call, or they never said ready in the first place.

### A3
Statement: We believe that without a named eval (what we re-measure after week one), people will treat "it worked in the demo" as enough to stop measuring.
Success criteria: No eval named: ≥4 of 5 say measurement can stop at demo. Eval named (one metric, one week-one check): ≥4 of 5 say measurement continues.
Failure criteria: Naming an eval does not change whether they stop at the demo.

## Slice(s)
### S1 (tests A1)
Method: One-page fake handoff. No Day-2 owner. Five people who know delivery. They write who owns drift. Then the same page with one named owner. Compare. Disposable. Do not build a support practice.
Type: Operational
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 5 people. 10 minutes.

### S2 (tests A2)
Method: Same five, later in the sitting. Handoff page with and without one untested abuse case. Ready / not ready. One variable only.
Type: Operational
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 10 minutes. Same people.

### S3 (tests A3)
Method: Same five. Demo-success page vs demo plus one week-one eval line. Do we keep measuring. One variable only.
Type: Operational
Execution: Requires manual run
Scope / time-box: 10 minutes. Same people.

## Signal
Success signal: S1 owner gap is visible, then closed by a name. S2 abuse case flips ready to not ready. S3 eval line keeps measurement on.
Failure signal: Any slice where the added line does not change the call, or people invent coverage that is not on the page.
Threshold: The register already marks this as a cluster. Fail on a slice → that slice stays open; do not treat Day-2 as one pass/fail. Pass on all three → this sample needs owner, one abuse case, and one eval named before handoff. That is not a full security or eval program. Do not bundle a fourth thing into these slices.
